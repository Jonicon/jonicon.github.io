About me, who am I?
Here will be a description of me and my history with miniature painting. Some opinion, too maybe.
I got hooked into Warhammer as a young kid. The usual story. Then years went by and I noticed that I had never played a single game, I was just painting.
It's now neem 25 years and I still have not played any games with my figures. I just paint.
And then at the end of 2022 I had the bright idea to start importing some hobby gear that I found useful, but no one else in Finland was selling. And that business is Ascetic Miniatures.

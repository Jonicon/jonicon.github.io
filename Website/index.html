New beginnings

This site is my modest input for the finnish miniature painting hobby scene.

While the online store is gone, I hope to continue to share what knowledge I have gathered over the years with other artists and hobbyists. Look forward into me posting either tutorials or opinion pieces regarding the hobby, the products that we use, or more philosophical works on art, be it miniature art or other wise.
